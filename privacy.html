Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Speaker Cleaner & Eject Water ("the App"), provided by EEB Consultancy LLC ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the App.

The App performs its core speaker cleaning and audio processing locally on your device. The free version also uses third-party advertising services, which may collect certain information as described below.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

We do not operate an account system or backend service for storing your speaker cleaning activity.

However, third-party advertising and consent management services integrated into the App may automatically collect certain device, usage, advertising, location, and diagnostic information.

Depending on the services used and applicable permissions, this information may include:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers when permitted.
  • Approximate location, including location inferred from an IP address.
  • Advertising data and information about interactions with ads.
  • Product interaction and usage information.
  • Crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic information.
  • Privacy preferences and consent choices needed to manage advertising.

The availability and use of particular identifiers and advertising features may depend on your device settings, location, consent choices, and applicable law.

2. Microphone Access

The App includes a Noise Meter feature that requires access to your device's microphone.

Microphone input is used only while you actively use the Noise Meter to estimate the surrounding sound level. Audio processing takes place on your device.

The App does not record, save, upload, transmit, or share microphone audio.

Microphone access is subject to your permission. You may deny or revoke microphone access through your device settings. Doing so will prevent the Noise Meter from functioning but will not prevent you from using features that do not require microphone access.

3. Speaker Cleaning and Audio Processing

Features such as Water Eject, Dust Cleaning, Auto Clean, Tone Generator, and Speaker Tests generate and process audio locally on your device.

Audio generated by these features is not uploaded to our servers or used to identify or track you.

4. Vibration Features

Vibration Cleaning uses supported device haptic functionality to generate vibration. This functionality is performed locally on your device and does not require us to collect personal information.

5. 7 Day Cleaner and Local Data

The 7 Day Cleaner may store limited routine information locally on your device, such as routine progress and reminder preferences.

This information is used to provide the feature and is not transmitted to EEB Consultancy LLC.

The App may also store local information needed to manage free feature allowances, rewarded advertising access, and advertising frequency limits.

6. Notifications

If you choose to enable the Daily Reminder, the App may request permission to send local notifications.

Notification permission is optional. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device and can be disabled in the App or through your device settings.

7. Purchases and Subscriptions

The App offers optional in-app purchases, including auto-renewable subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not directly collect, process, or store your payment card information, billing credentials, or Apple Account password.

The App may receive information from StoreKit necessary to determine purchase and subscription status, including whether a purchase is active, expired, revoked, or otherwise entitled to Premium access.

Premium access removes advertising within the App. The App is designed not to initialize its advertising services for users with active Premium access.

Subscription billing, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and payment processing are handled by Apple and are subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

8. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The free version of the App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

Advertisements may include banner ads, interstitial ads, and optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads allow users to access certain features without purchasing Premium.

Google Mobile Ads may collect device identifiers, approximate location, advertising data, product interactions, crash information, performance information, and other diagnostic data.

Such information may be used for advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, service functionality, and related purposes described in Google's privacy documentation.

Some information may be associated with device identifiers or other identifiers. Other diagnostic information may be processed without being linked to your identity.

The App also uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent and privacy choices. UMP may process information needed to present consent messages, record privacy choices, and support consent management.

Where required, the App requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before allowing tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies.

If you decline tracking permission, the App does not obtain permission to access the advertising identifier for tracking. Advertisements may still be displayed using non-personalized or limited advertising methods, where permitted.

Declining tracking permission does not prevent you from using the App's core features.

Advertising data practices may vary depending on your region, privacy choices, device settings, and applicable law.

We do not operate our own cross-app tracking system or sell personal information directly. However, advertising partners may process information for advertising and tracking purposes as described in their own privacy policies and subject to applicable permissions and legal requirements.

9. Your Advertising Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable regulations, the App may present a consent form that allows you to accept, decline, or manage certain advertising data processing activities.

Where applicable, you may review or change your advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Options section in the App's Settings.

You can also manage tracking permission through your device's privacy settings.

Choosing not to consent to personalized advertising or tracking does not necessarily remove all advertisements. Limited or non-personalized advertisements may still appear.

Purchasing Premium removes in-app advertisements.

10. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Advertising delivery, measurement, and related services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Advertising consent and privacy preference management.
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit: Purchase processing, subscription management, and entitlement verification.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal obligations.

You can learn more about their practices here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads Data Disclosure

11. Data Retention

We do not maintain our own server-side records of microphone audio, speaker cleaning activity, or user accounts.

Information stored locally by the App generally remains on your device until it is reset, removed by the App, or deleted as part of removing the App, subject to device and operating system behavior.

Google and other third-party service providers may retain advertising, consent, usage, and diagnostic information according to their own retention policies and applicable law.

Purchase and subscription records maintained by Apple are subject to Apple's data retention practices.

12. Children's Privacy

The App does not require children or other users to create an account or provide personal contact information to us.

The App includes third-party advertising services, which may process information as described in this Privacy Policy. Advertising and consent practices are subject to applicable age-related requirements, platform settings, and privacy laws.

Parents and guardians can use available device settings and parental controls to manage purchases, permissions, and other device functionality.

No device, software, or electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
